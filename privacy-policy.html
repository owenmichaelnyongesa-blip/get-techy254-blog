1. Introduction

Welcome to Get.Techy254 ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at get-techy254-blog.vercel.app (the "Site").

By using this Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

Get.Techy254 is operated by Michael Owen Nyongesa, based in Nairobi, Kenya.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information:

Information you provide directly:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This is stored securely in our Supabase database.
  • Contact enquiries: If you contact us via email, we retain your name and email address to respond to your enquiry.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and browser/device type. This is collected via analytics tools (see Section 4).
  • IP address: Your approximate location may be inferred from your IP address for analytics purposes.
  • Cookies: Small text files placed on your device to improve your experience and enable advertising (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver and improve the content on this Site
  • Send newsletters to subscribers who have opted in

  • Display relevant advertisements to support the free operation of this blog
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us and our advertising partners recognise your browser and remember certain information.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the Site to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand visitor behaviour and improve the Site. These may include tools such as Google Analytics.
  • Advertising cookies: Used to display relevant ads and measure their effectiveness. These are set by our advertising partners (see Section 5).

Managing cookies: You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect your experience on this and other websites. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones under Settings → Privacy.

5. Advertising

This Site displays advertisements to support its free operation. We work with advertising networks that may use cookies and similar technologies to show you relevant ads based on your interests and browsing behaviour.

Our advertising partners may include:

  • Ezoic — an AI-driven ad platform that uses cookies to personalise and optimise ad display. Ezoic's privacy policy is available at ezoic.com/privacy-policy.
  • Google AdSense / Google Ad Manager — Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

These advertising partners operate independently and have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data. We encourage you to review their policies.

To opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies, you may also visit aboutads.info/choices or networkadvertising.org/choices.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at get.techy254@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any newsletter email.
